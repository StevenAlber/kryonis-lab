Institutional Engagement · SVC-01

Sovereign BioCapital Assessment.

A structured engagement that positions a territory's biological capital for institutional visibility, verification, and capital entry — before any measurement is commissioned. It does not measure the territory. It establishes where the territory stands, what is missing, and the defensible pathway to capital entry.

The Problem

Measured but invisible. Verified but unrecorded. Valuable but unbanked.

A sovereign or institutional holder of territory sits on biological capital it cannot yet see on its own balance sheet: peatland carbon, boreal biomass, freshwater systems, biodiversity, permafrost. The barrier is not the absence of biology. It is the absence of a defensible structure through which that biology becomes legible to institutional capital.

No existing instrument answers, in one defensible document, the four questions an institutional counterparty asks before it commits capital:

Identity

Is the asset real and identifiable?

Does it carry a unique, jurisdictionally-anchored identity (BAIN ID) and a defined boundary?

State

What clearing-state is it in?

Observed, measured, verified, registered, or capital-ready — BCCS clearing-states 1 through 5.

Gap

What is missing to advance it?

The precise evidentiary and structural gap between its current state and capital entry.

Pathway

What is the defensible pathway?

The sequence — not a promise — of what must be measured, verified, and legally encapsulated, and in what order.

Deliverables

What the client receives

Every engagement delivers a written institutional record, not a slide deck.

01

Territorial Position Brief

The territory placed in the BCCS clearing-state framework, with provisional BAIN identifier and boundary definition.

02

Evidentiary Gap Map

A structured account of what stands between the territory's current state and capital eligibility, by asset layer.

03

Clearing Pathway

The defensible sequence to advance clearing-state — including what measurement and verification each step requires, and who must perform it.

04

Capital-Readiness Statement

A sober assessment of what an institutional counterparty would require before capital entry, and the legal-vehicle considerations involved.

05

Doctrine Annex

The KRYONIS verification-first framework (R-series standard) against which the position is constructed — so the document is self-justifying to an expert reader.

Boundaries

What this engagement is not

Not measurement

No satellite or ground survey is performed. The Assessment defines what must be measured; instrument-led measurement is a distinct subsequent stage, performed by qualified parties within the structure this engagement establishes.

Not a carbon-credit service

Carbon is one asset layer among several. The Assessment positions territorial bio-capital as a whole; it does not issue, broker, or value credits.

Not tokenisation

No token is issued or implied. Tokenisation, if it ever occurs, is downstream of clearing-state 5 and outside this engagement.

Not advisory-by-the-hour

A fixed-scope, fixed-fee institutional deliverable with a defined beginning and end.
